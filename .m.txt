store: skip the migration body when the schema is already current

`migrate` ran 28 `CREATE ... IF NOT EXISTS` statements plus a dozen column probes on every
open, then called `init_schema`, then returned `Ok(true)` regardless of whether anything had
changed. Nothing recorded that the shape was current, so the work repeated forever.

A write transaction costs far more on a large database with a dirty WAL than on a small one, so
the same fixed work cost 6.7 ms at 100 commits and 31 ms at 1000. Traced through
`GESTA_TRACE_OPEN`, that was the whole of the long-history slope: `open_db` was flat at 9-10 ms
in both, `commit_one` was flat at 2.5 and 3.8 ms, and only `migrate` moved.

It now reads `PRAGMA user_version` and returns early when the shape is current, stamping the
value after the body runs. Measured after the change: 1.7 ms on both fixtures, first open
4.8 ms then 1.7 ms on every open after.

`bench/long_history.py`: 0.89x and 0.27x across two runs, against 3.09x in the gate before.
